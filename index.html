Seven Star Printing Press was founded on a simple belief: every business deserves print materials that make a lasting impression. We combine modern printing technology with meticulous attention to detail.

Based locally and serving clients nationally, we specialize in custom sticker cutting, laser cutting, T-shirt printing, and premium acrylic nameplates — tailored to your exact specifications.

Our Services

01
✂️
Sticker Cutting

Custom die-cut and kiss-cut stickers in any shape. Vinyl-grade durability for indoor and outdoor use.

  • Kiss-cut & die-cut options
  • Waterproof vinyl material
  • Gloss, matte & clear finishes
  • Full CMYK colour printing
02
🔆
Laser Cutting

Precision laser cutting for wood, acrylic, cardstock, and leather. Complex shapes with hair-line accuracy.

  • 0.1mm precision tolerance
  • Acrylic, wood, leather, card
  • Engraving & etching
  • Custom sizes up to A0
03
👕
T-shirt Printing

DTG, screen printing, and heat transfer. Vivid, wash-fast colours on any garment — perfect for teams and events.

  • DTG, screen print & heat transfer
  • All fabric types accepted
  • Front, back & sleeve prints
  • Pantone colour matching
04
🏷️
Acrylic Nameplates

Premium engraved acrylic nameplates for offices, doors, and desks. Polished edges and UV-stable inks.

  • 5mm cast acrylic standard
  • Multiple colour options
  • Laser engraved text & logo
  • Wall-mount hardware included
